> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. Invented for testing. Not real Salesforce data.

## Why we segment

Accounts are grouped into segments so that each one gets a sales motion and a coverage model that fits its size and complexity. Segment is assigned once a year during planning and only changes mid-year through the exception process below.

## Segment bands

| Segment | Employee count | Primary motion |
|---|---|---|
| Small Business | 1-199 | Inside sales, high velocity |
| Commercial | 200-1,499 | Inside sales with field support |
| Enterprise | 1,500+ | Field sales, named accounts |

Employee count comes from the account's most recent firmographic refresh. If two sources disagree, the higher count wins.

## Exceptions

- Strategic logos can be moved up one segment with VP approval.
- Public-sector accounts are always covered by the public-sector team, whatever their size.
- Contact sales ops for exceptions that are not listed here.
