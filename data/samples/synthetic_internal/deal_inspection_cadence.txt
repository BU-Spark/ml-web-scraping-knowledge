# SYNTHETIC Deal inspection cadence

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. Invented for testing. Not real Salesforce data.

## Weekly forecast call

Every Monday, first-line managers review all opportunities expected to close this quarter. Reps update the next step, close date and qualification fields before the call.

## Qualification criteria by stage

- Stage 2: a pain is confirmed and an economic buyer is identified.
- Stage 3: decision criteria and decision process are documented.
- Stage 4: a mutual close plan is agreed with the champion.

## Quarterly business review

At the end of each quarter, managers compare forecast accuracy against actual bookings and pick two deals to review in depth with the team.
