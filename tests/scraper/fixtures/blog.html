What Is MEDDICC? A Practical Guide

MEDDICC is a sales qualification methodology that helps sellers decide which opportunities deserve their time and how to win them. Teams that sell complex software to large companies use it to keep deal reviews honest and consistent across the whole organization.

The seven elements

Each letter stands for one thing the seller must understand about the deal before forecasting it with confidence. Missing any of them is a signal that the opportunity carries more risk than the forecast suggests.

  • Metrics: the measurable value the customer expects to gain.
  • Economic buyer: the person with final authority over the budget.
  • Decision criteria: how the customer will compare the options.

Metrics in practice

Good metrics are specific and owned by the customer. A seller should be able to state the expected gain in the customer's own words and connect it to a business outcome that an executive already cares about deeply.

When to use MEDDICC

MEDDICC fits long, multi-stakeholder enterprise deals better than fast transactional sales. Small deals that close in a single call rarely need the full framework, while large enterprise deals with procurement and legal review benefit from every element being documented.
